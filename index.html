Settings & backup

Changes are saved to your Google Sheet when you press Save. Cover photos are stored in your Google Drive in the folder “RahDas Bookshelf Covers”.

Daily reminder

Get an email in the evening on days you haven’t logged any reading — with your current book, this month’s pages and your streak. Allow notifications for your mail app on your phone to see it as a notification.

Install the free ntfy app (iPhone or Android), tap +, subscribe to the same topic name. Keep the name secret – anyone who knows it can read your reminders.
Phone notification not arriving (error 429)?
The free ntfy.sh server sometimes refuses Google’s servers. Fix: make a free account at ntfy.sh (in a browser), open Account → Access tokens → Create token, and paste the token (starts with tk_) here. Your topic stays the same.
Telegram (reliable alternative to ntfy)
1) In Telegram, search @BotFather, send /newbot, choose a name, and copy the token it gives you. 2) Open your new bot, tap Start and send “hi”. 3) Paste the token below and tap Save reminder – the app finds your chat by itself. Telegram then shows a normal popup on your phone.
WhatsApp (optional, uses the free CallMeBot service)
One-time setup: save +34 644 51 95 23 in your contacts, send it the WhatsApp message “I allow callmebot to send me messages”, and it replies with your key. Enter your number with country code and the key here. CallMeBot is a third-party service and can change or stop.

Works with no internet. It adds a daily repeating event with a notification at the time above to your phone’s calendar. Open the downloaded file and tap Add / Save. To stop it, delete the event in your calendar.

This app

Installed as an app on this device. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: menu ⋮ → Install app. Your saved shelf stays viewable offline.

Your shelf

Your name shows on the home page: type Rahdas and it reads “Rahdas Bookshelf”. Leave it empty for “My Bookshelf”. The photo goes into the picture frame on the wall of your shelf. Saved to your Sheet, so it is the same on every device.

Reading targets

Tick a target to show it on the home page; untick to hide it. The reminder email and the home page use these numbers to tell you how many pages to read today.

Book popup fields

Choose what shows when you tap a book’s cover. Saved to your Sheet, so it is the same on every device.

English titles

For existing books (paper and PDF) with a Tamil title or author, fills the empty English title, Title meaning and Author in English columns. It never overwrites anything you already typed. Machine translations can be literal, so edit any you want to change.
